## 2. Production Readiness Verdict

- **CURRENT STATUS**: **PILOT / PROTOTYPE READINESS (AFTER FIXING CRITICAL & HIGH ISSUES)**
- **OFFICIAL RAILWAY PRODUCTION STATUS**: **NOT READY (REQUIRES AUTHORITATIVE INTEGRATIONS)**
- **Explanation**: The system possesses excellent mathematical and architectural foundations, but critical shortcuts in the primary user workflow (bypassing CP-SAT in coordinated planning, fake replanning, fake what-if, 00:00–24:00 false availability) must be fixed before any field trial. Furthermore, a real-world railway deployment requires official organizational integration with CRIS, BDMS, and Indian Railways secure intranet infrastructure.
